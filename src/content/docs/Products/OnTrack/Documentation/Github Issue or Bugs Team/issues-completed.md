## For the future

### Issue \#1265

As of now the waveform when recording audio works, along with tooltips and more. To expand on this
for the version 11 of OnTrack we could do something similar for the playback of audio snippets in
the chat window. Right now they are just a simple line that shows the progression of audio playing
not the waveform.

Potential iteration could include:

- Displaying the waveform of an audio snippet in the chat window.
- Highlighting the portion of the waveform that has already been played.
- Allowing users to click on the waveform to seek through the audio.
- Reusing existing waveform components or logic from the recording interface where possible.
- Investigating how waveforms should be generated.
- Ensuring the waveform works with different audio formats and lengths.
